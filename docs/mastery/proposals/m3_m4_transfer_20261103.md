## M3-A / T180: stability across depth

**Question:** does a normalization/residual change preserve useful signal and
trainability as depth increases under the declared recipe, and after equal-effort
retuning with explicitly nonmatched compute? Connect to [5.1 modern decoder implementation](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter5_modern_architectures/master_5_1.py)
and [17.4 cross-architecture diagnostics](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter17_training_dynamics/master_17_4.py);
neither lesson's historical output proves this new comparison.

**Cold open, analytic only:** in scalar residual blocks
`h_next = h + a*h`, four blocks with `h0=1` yield `1.1**4 = 1.4641` for `a=.1`
and `1.5**4 = 5.0625` for `a=.5`; the input derivative has the same factor.
An identity branch (`a=0`) yields one. Predict the consequence of changing depth
before seeing the formula evaluated. This establishes multiplicative propagation,
not a theorem about normalized transformers or training quality.

**Finite design:** baseline plus two single-component arms: normalization off,
and changed residual scaling. Freeze exact layer/operator placement and the
second scale from the accepted architecture contract; do not quietly change both
in one arm. Use one exposed depth and one unseen depth from the approved family.
A nonapplicable component is a design rejection, not an invented implementation.

**Student work:** `residual_step(h, branch, scale)`,
`layerwise_rms(activations)`, `gradient_rms(parameters_by_layer)`,
`assert_fixed_settings(baseline, arm, allowed_diff)` and
`align_training_traces(traces, budget_axis)`. Immediate tests check scalar
identity, the two exact factors, per-layer reduction axes, missing-gradient
versus zero-gradient distinction, input preservation and rejection of an
accidentally changed learning rate. Student instrumentation must consume actual
layers/gradients; a fixture cannot fabricate a real trace.

**Required visible result (unmeasured):** layer/depth activation and gradient RMS
panels beside seed-level loss versus tokens and actual compute; distinguish
initialization, training checkpoints, nonfinite failure and censoring. No required
winning arm or invented loss threshold. Expected analytic output is exactly the
factors above; expected empirical signature is correctly aligned measured traces
that can support stability, instability or an inconclusive difference.

**Controls/play/transfer:** identity residual is the analytic no-change control;
unchanged cloned configuration is the empirical replay baseline. Compare loss to
norms so bounded norms cannot masquerade as learning. Try changing one residual
scale and explaining which panel changed. Then lock predictions for the unseen
depth, including whether the fixed-recipe ordering survives equal-effort retuning;
compute matching remains outside the proposed grid.
False positives: vanishing activations presented as healthy because they are
bounded; missing gradients plotted as zero; a stable but untrained model declared
better; or selective removal of divergent seeds.

## M3-B / T181: capacity under an explicit budget

**Question:** does extending one existing component improve loss when the
comparison budget is stated correctly? Reuse the M2 family and
[17.4 accounting/representation bridge](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter17_training_dynamics/master_17_4.py),
not a new architecture survey.

**Cold open, analytic only:** a biased two-layer MLP with input/output width `d`
and hidden width `h` has `2*d*h+h+d` parameters. With `d=4`, `h=4` gives 40 and
`h=8` gives 76. Equal steps/tokens do not imply equal matrix-multiplication cost.
These are illustrative counts, not measured hardware throughput or model results.

**Finite design:** baseline and one MLP-inner-width extension at an exposed budget;
transfer to one approved unseen budget. If the chosen M2 organism lacks this
component, #33 must explicitly approve the corresponding existing component
before implementation. For the current proposed M2 grid, show equal-token fixed
recipe separately from equal-candidate/seed/update/token retuning effort, with
nonmatched compute explicit in both. A compute-matched retuning panel remains a
separate deferred protocol/cap decision; the proposed 82 fits cannot establish it.
Name each resource axis and its estimand.

**Student work:** `count_unique_parameters(named_parameters)`,
`mlp_parameter_count(d, h)`, `count_processed_tokens(batches)`,
`sum_actual_cost(ledger)` and `match_budget(traces, ceiling)`. Tests require 40/76,
no double-counting tied parameters, masks/padding excluded under the frozen token
convention, inclusion of failed/tuning/control runs and no silent extrapolation
past an observed trace. A tiny literal ledger tests arithmetic without training.

**Required visible result (unmeasured):** loss versus tokens and actual compute,
with parameter counts and each seed displayed; an intentionally mismatched-budget
panel explicitly labeled misleading, followed by its corrected comparison. No
assumption of monotonic improvement. Controls are the identical baseline replay,
identical-data schedule where intended, and the deliberately unequal-budget foil.
Try It Yourself changes the comparison axis and states the changed question.
The unseen challenge predicts whether the apparent capacity gain survives a new
budget and the declared retuning allowance. False positives: extra tokens sold as
architecture gain, an uncharged search, duplicated shared weights, or an
interpolated DEV fit called independent extrapolation.

## M4-A / T183: predictive information versus causal intervention

**Question:** does a feature that predicts behavior actually control it under a
specified intervention? Link [7.1 lenses/Patchscopes](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter7_activation_to_language/master_7_1.py),
[8.1 patching](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter8_automated_circuits/master_8_1.py),
[6.1 SAE controls](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter6_sparse_feature_methods/master_6_1.py)
and [11.1 geometry](../../../infrastructure/chapters/chapter11_representation_geometry/master_11_1.py).
These are source connections; this slice does not claim to complete every linked
method or introduce a new sparse-feature training campaign.

**Cold open, exact counterexample:** let `h=(s,s)`, `s` in `{-2,2}`, and the output
read only `h[1]`. A probe of `h[0]` predicts perfectly. Starting at `(2,2)`, replace
only `h[0]` by donor value `-2`: output remains 2. Replace `h[1]`: output becomes
-2. Prediction alone did not identify the readout's causal coordinate.

**Finite real design:** use one already approved M2 checkpoint and one registered
DEV-selected feature/site; select nothing on confirmation. Fit the predictor on
TRAIN, select on DEV, and freeze donor/cohort/site/metric before held-out
interventions. Student functions: `fit_train_only_probe`,
`replace_activation_coordinates`, `paired_behavior_effect` and
`same_size_random_masks`. Immediate tests reproduce the exact 2/2/-2 example,
identity/no-hook equivalence, donor-row/site alignment, unchanged unpatched
coordinates and random-mask size. Reuse visible exercise functions rather than
hiding patching behind a runner.

**Required visible result (unmeasured):** prediction score beside paired causal
effects, identity/full-donor controls and same-size random controls, with generic
loss/capability damage alongside the target effect. Record all registered random
draws and seed-level results. Recovery ratios require a frozen finite positive
baseline gap; report raw effects and denominator rather than using epsilon,
clamping, dropping rows or claiming every valid effect must be in [0,1].
A predictive feature can have null intervention effect; a damaging intervention
can have broad effects without specificity. Both are interpretable outcomes.

Try It Yourself changes a DEV site or donor and explains the causal estimand.
Transfer freezes a prediction on disjoint held-out prompts/conditions, not an
already exposed Q006 public example. A changed layer, direction or mask is a new
attempt. False positives: probe accuracy called causality; arbitrary scale damage;
choosing only successful prompts; shuffled directions with equally large effects;
or a public/replayed condition called unseen. ACDC integration remains behind
D04/D06 and owner #17, not smuggled in as this exercise's default.
