### Intended learner outcomes

A learner should be able to choose the right quantity before plotting, derive and implement the central operation, predict what a control will do, recognize a failed assumption, distinguish a recovered description from causal use, and transfer the reasoning to an unrevealed changed case. The course must develop these abilities through concrete examples and progressively reduced assistance. A checklist, confidence score, polished scatterplot or report cannot substitute for that reasoning.

Every new or revised lab follows: **question and concrete example → explanation/equations/shapes → forecast with confidence → visible student core → immediate independent checks → signature output with controls → interpretation → editable play → changed-case forecast and reflection**. Give separate hints, help, interpretation and complete solutions. The method taught stays inside learner-visible notebook cells; shared utilities may load, display and record. Retain faithful nulls and failed controls. Reference figures are guided practice and count as exposure.

Dependencies: quantities precede losses and PCA; losses plus PCA precede nonlinear views; quantities/losses/PCA precede metric interpretation; quantities precede inference. Geometry, metrics and inference precede PRH claims; losses/PCA/metrics precede JEPA comparisons. A diagnostic asks the learner to calculate an asymmetric KL, recover the PCA counterexample, distinguish t-SNE/UMAP quantities, distinguish missing/false neighbors, apply Bonferroni with its conditions, and explain why a good plot/probe/loss need not establish causal use. Save assistance/exposure and the remediation route. A code pass or high confidence does not establish understanding.

**Exact foundation anchors and conditions.** For normalized P,Q and natural logs, KL(P‖Q)=Σp log(p/q)=CE(P,Q)−H(P); zero p contributes zero and positive p with zero q gives infinity. KL is directional and not a metric; CE/KL minimization agrees only for fixed P. Our worked arithmetic: P=(.9,.1), Q=(.5,.5) gives H=.325083, CE=.693147, KL(P‖Q)=.368064 and KL(Q‖P)=.510826 nats. Ask for a new Q before revealing its result. For nonzero h=2t, cosine loss is zero while mean squared error is ‖t‖²/d; latent agreement alone can admit collapse. These examples are our derivations, not paper measurements. [Official entropy definitions](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.entropy.html).

For centered X=UΣVᵀ, scores XV_k reconstruct as XV_kV_kᵀ; covariance eigenvalues use σ²/(n−1), retained variance uses retained σ²/total σ², and omitted squared singular values equal squared reconstruction error. Centering, feature standardization, row normalization and whitening are different choices. Our four rows (−2,−1),(−2,1),(2,−1),(2,1) have singular values 4,2; rank one retains .8 variance with squared error 4, yet removes labels sign(y). Changing y's units changes that answer. Fit learned preprocessing on TRAIN; select rank on DEV using the intended task and neighborhood/reconstruction evidence. No universal 95%-variance or 50-PC rule is adopted. [Official PCA definition](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.decomposition.PCA.html).

For t-SNE, derive Gaussian conditional affinities, symmetrize as p_ij=(p_j|i+p_i|j)/(2n), set diagonals to zero, normalize Student-t affinities over ordered non-self pairs and minimize Σp_ij log(p_ij/q_ij). Perplexity exp(H(P_i)) describes effective neighbors, not cluster count. Four equal neighbors give perplexity four. The heavy tail addresses crowding; distant map gaps are not calibrated input distances. [Original t-SNE paper, equations 1–5](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume9/vandermaaten08a/vandermaaten08a.pdf).

For UMAP, teach local memberships on retained neighbor edges, fuzzy union μ_ij=w_i→j+w_j→i−w_i→jw_j→i and ν_ij=(1+a‖y_i−y_j‖^(2b))⁻¹. Directed memberships .8,.5 yield .9, not 1.3. These edge strengths do not globally sum to one. The idealized edgewise loss −Σ_i<j[μ logν+(1−μ)log(1−ν)] is binary edge cross-entropy up to fixed target terms, not t-SNE's categorical KL. Distinguish it from a negative-sampled implementation's effective objective. Explain local-scale adaptation and sparse optimization, then measure usefulness against the task; **UMAP is not universally best**. PCA, t-SNE, original-space heatmaps/neighbors and direct coordinate views are genuine comparators. Neither density, area, orientation nor gap size alone proves population structure. [UMAP paper](https://arxiv.org/html/1802.03426), [implementation-objective analysis](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/file/2de5d16682c3c35007e4e92982f1a2ba-Paper.pdf), [official clustering cautions](https://umap-learn.readthedocs.io/en/latest/clustering.html).

Trustworthiness penalizes introduced display neighbors; continuity penalizes lost original neighbors. Declare input metric, k, ranks, ties and coverage. Neither measures semantic truth or causality. Report original→intermediate and original→final diagnostics, including seed/settings variability in neighbor sets. Random labels should leave geometry unchanged while removing label evidence in expectation. Metric contracts state denominator, grouping, direction, aggregation and uncertainty. Our 95-negative/5-positive majority predictor has 95% accuracy and zero positive recall; identical hard predictions can have different probability loss. Reliability diagrams need bin counts and do not turn Brier into a pure calibration measure. [Trustworthiness definition](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.manifold.trustworthiness.html), [continuity/evaluation paper](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume11/venna10a/venna10a.pdf), [calibration paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a/guo17a.pdf).

Bonferroni rejects valid individual p-values at α/m or adjusts by min(1,mp). The union bound gives FWER≤α without independence, for the declared family. Our α=.05,m=4,p=(.004,.02,.03,.2) gives threshold .0125 and adjusted (.016,.08,.12,.8). Independent all-null uncorrected testing has FWER=1−.95^m; four identical valid tests have .05 instead. FWER and FDR differ. Valid-p correction does not repair invalid tests, optional stopping, omitted searches or invalid exchangeability. Layers, metrics, seeds, prompts and outcome-driven retries belong in the family record. Pairwise distances and permutation replicates are not independent subjects. Teach effect size, intervals, paired contrasts, independent units, power and exploratory/confirmatory distinction alongside arithmetic. [NIST Bonferroni derivation](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc473.htm), [original FDR paper](https://www.stat.purdue.edu/~doerge/BIOINFORM.D/FALL06/Benjamini%20and%20Y%20FDR.pdf), [ML pitfalls guide](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.02497).

### Just-in-time loss map, rather than a catalog to memorize

| Existing question | Objective to unpack | One decisive distinction / proposed control |
|---|---|---|
| Token/class probability | CE and KL above | Target support, direction and reduction matter; compare constant probabilities and equally accurate predictors. |
| Predict a continuous latent target | MSE or cosine above, as specified by the actual recipe | Magnitude error vs angular error; rescale predictions, and inspect constant-output collapse. |
| Identify the paired text/image or prediction/target among candidates | Paired InfoNCE below | Positive identity, negative sampling and temperature define the task; shuffle pair IDs and insert a semantically duplicate negative. |
| Prevent trivial representations in an applicable self-supervised recipe | Advanced variance/covariance regularization | Inspect batchwise standard deviations and off-diagonal covariance; do not equate a noncollapsed batch with useful representations. |

For a batch of B aligned pairs, let normalized vectors u_i and v_i have scores \(s_{ij}=u_i^Tv_j\), temperature τ>0, one designated positive j=i, and negatives j≠i. A row-direction paired InfoNCE loss is

\[
\ell_i^{u\to v}=-\log\frac{\exp(s_{ii}/\tau)}{\sum_{j=1}^{B}\exp(s_{ij}/\tau)},\qquad
L_{sym}=\frac1{2B}\sum_i(\ell_i^{u\to v}+\ell_i^{v\to u}).
\]

The denominator includes the positive. Bidirectionality swaps the candidate/query roles, equivalently the score-matrix axes. [CPC, §2.3, equation 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.03748) introduces InfoNCE; [CLIP, §2.3 and Figure 3](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/radford21a/radford21a.pdf) supplies the symmetric normalized image/text recipe. The [VL-JEPA v1 methodology](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.10942v1#S2) explicitly adopts bidirectional InfoNCE; the JEPA name alone does not imply MSE, negatives, EMA targets, or a universal anti-collapse mechanism.

**Own exact proposed mini-example:** B=2 with all four scores equal gives each directional loss log 2. If diagonal scores are 1 and off-diagonals 0, each is \(\log(1+e^{-1/\tau})\). Forecast what decreasing τ does when a wrong pair instead has the larger score; then inspect per-pair contributions. A high-scoring negative may be a valid alternate caption, not a truly dissimilar item: false negatives are a substantive contrastive-learning issue, not a code failure. [Chuang et al. 2020, abstract and §2](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/file/63c3ddcc7b23daa1e42dc41f9a44a873-Paper.pdf). This example is a design, not an executed contrastive experiment.

Advanced recipe-specific connection: VICReg combines an invariance term with batchwise standard-deviation and off-diagonal-covariance regularizers. [Bardes et al., §4.1, equations 1–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2105.04906v3#S4.SS1). Explain which axis is the batch and what a constant representation does to each term; do not transfer the recipe or an “EMA prevents collapse” claim to all JEPAs. MAE/Huber robust-regression choices stay deferred unless an actual numeric world-state target, units and outlier/error model make that comparison relevant.


### Where the other visualization techniques belong

This is a decision aid inside F2–F4, not new chapters or a requirement to implement every method. “Preserved quantity” means the method's target or representation, not a guarantee of perfect preservation in two dimensions. Controls below are proposed discriminating exercises; no method wins by plot appearance.

| View | Target question / quantity | Failure to recognize / proposed control |
|---|---|---|
| Raw scatter and pair plots | Which measured coordinates vary together, in their actual units? | Pairwise views can miss multivariate relationships; compare the same IDs across coordinates and a shuffled-coordinate view. |
| Distance / Gram heatmap | Which original-space pairs are close, or have large inner products under the declared normalization/kernel? | Row order can manufacture blocks; permute display order while retaining IDs, compare raw/normalized vectors and pairwise-distance vs similarity definitions. Gram entries are inner products, not automatically distances. |
| PCA | Which linear subspace retains squared variation/reconstruction? | High-variance nuisance can dominate; use the rectangle's low-variance label and a no-reduction baseline. |
| Random projection | Does a learned projection beat a simple geometry baseline? | Distance guarantees require sufficient dimension and stated distortion/probability conditions; two plotting axes need not satisfy them. Compare distance distortion and neighborhood retention across declared seeds/dimensions. [Official guide, §8.6.1](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/random_projection.html). |
| Metric MDS | Can display distances approximate the supplied dissimilarities? Raw stress is \(\sum_{i<j}(\delta_{ij}-\|z_i-z_j\|)^2\). | Stress can hide which distances distort and depends on scale; show a Shepard distance plot/residuals and an exact small Euclidean configuration. Classical MDS uses a different strain objective; do not conflate it with stress minimization. [Official MDS guide, §2.2.8](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/manifold.html#multidimensional-scaling). |
| Isomap | Can graph shortest-path distances approximate manifold geodesics before embedding? | A shortcut edge can collapse the intended long route; disconnects also matter. Add/remove one bridge on a curved strip and compare graph paths with ambient Euclidean distances. [Official Isomap guide and practical cautions](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/manifold.html#isomap). |
| Laplacian / spectral embedding | Which coordinates vary smoothly over a chosen weighted neighborhood graph? | Graph construction/components and near-degenerate eigenvalues can destabilize coordinates; inspect components, eigengaps and neighbor perturbations, comparing subspaces rather than eigenvector signs. An eigengap is a diagnostic, not automatic proof of intrinsic dimension. [Official spectral embedding reference](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.manifold.SpectralEmbedding.html). |
| Diffusion maps | Which points have similar t-step transition distributions for a declared Markov operator? Coordinates weight eigenvectors by eigenvalues raised to t; truncation approximates the associated diffusion geometry. | Kernel normalization, bandwidth and diffusion time change the geometry; compare t and graph construction, including a bottleneck control. Diffusion distance is not ordinary Euclidean distance, UMAP's edge loss, or automatically the PRH comparison kernel. [Coifman & Lafon 2006, §2.2–2.5](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~strohmer/courses/270/diffusion_maps.pdf). |
| t-SNE / UMAP | Which neighborhood-affinity relations can this map communicate? | Attractive islands can mislead about density or global distance; use F3's original-neighbor views, nulls and parameter/seed controls. See the original papers and official cautions above. |

The graph, Markov operator, pairwise distances and Gram matrix are separate objects. A learner moving toward PRH/kernel alignment must write down which one is being compared and why, before selecting a display. Geodesic/spectral/diffusion implementations stay advanced unless an existing lesson needs that target question.


**Named PRH research chapter (PRH01–PRH06).** Title proposal: *The Platonic Representation Hypothesis: What Converges, Why, and When?* Reserve its number until canonical placement is decided. Begin after geometry/alignment; revisit its JEPA theorem section after I-JEPA/family prerequisites, avoiding a cycle. The original hypothesis, critical reassessment, response and constructive transfer each receive a substantive lab. The intuition is that shared views reward some distinctions, anti-collapse constraints prevent trivial agreement, geometry measures compatible distinctions, and interventions test behavioral use. This is an explanatory spine, not one universal architecture theorem.

The [original PRH paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.07987v5) and [author explainer](https://phillipi.github.io/prh/) motivate relations between corresponding examples, empirical alignment and an idealized contrastive account; they do not assert neuron identity. The [Aristotelian reassessment](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.14486v2) motivates width/search calibration and local/global analysis; [Structure over Geometry](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27252v1) separates structural from metric agreement. Teach their competing operationalizations rather than selecting a winner by rhetoric. The [unpaired Rosetta Stone method](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09411v2) estimates correspondence as well as a map; measuring alignment with known pairs is a different task.

For the [exact-title JEPA follow-up](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.39302v1), explicitly list finite world, injective renderings, common symmetric positives/marginals, population minimizers, rotation-invariant loss/anti-collapse and rotation-unique solutions. Its identity-predictor theorem excludes learned/action-conditioned predictors. Whitened-MSE uniqueness needs an eigengap; spectral mismatch bounds need their actual norms/weights. Rotation existence does not identify the map. Unequal-dimension empirical maps, empirical SIGReg training and broad arbitrary LLMs are separate claims; the paper says no experiment exactly meets its theorem. Its grouping/recaption protocol supplies information and cannot be advertised as pair-free emergence. Author-reported sample efficiency and model-size comparisons remain literature results until separately replicated. [LeJEPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08544v3) uses predictive agreement plus SIGReg without a generic EMA/stop-gradient recipe. The [deep-linear perfect-PRH result](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01098v2) is another scoped setting, not interchangeable proof.
