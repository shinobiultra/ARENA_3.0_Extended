# Which tools do the lessons use before teaching their intuition? (Issue #241)

Status: research inventory, 2026-11-06. Facts only; placement and depth belong to the foundations ticket this one blocks (#234). Throwaway branch, never merged. Nothing here was executed; this is a reading and search exercise over instruction pages.

## How to read the table

- **Lessons**: the 86 sections in `infrastructure/extension/syllabus.yaml` (chapters 5-18, plus 0.6 and 1.6), matched to one page each (86/86 matched: exact title, or a unique title prefix when the file name is shortened). Where a stale duplicate page exists (for example 5.2 has a Gemma Scope page and a MoE page), the page whose title matches the syllabus was used. For 0.6 and 1.6 that is the `..._Practice.md` page.
- **Order** is syllabus order (0.6, 1.6, 5.1 ... 18.2). "First use" is the first lesson in that order where the tool is used substantively, not merely named in a config key or reference list (examples: KL first matches 1.6 only as `kl_divergence=1e-10`; "Jensen" in 11.4 is Jensen's inequality, not JS divergence).
- **Counts** are numbers of lessons whose page matches a hand-tuned regex. They overcount (reference lists, passing mentions) and undercount (synonyms). Two early regexes were wrong (case-insensitive `ROC` matched "process", `ridge` matched "bridge") and were replaced by word-bounded ones; only the corrected counts appear below.
- **Evidence tag** per row: **R** = I read the surrounding passage (cited by lesson and line); **G** = grep/regex and headings only, so the "intuition" verdict is a weaker inference.
- **Intuition verdict** scale: **Built** = a picture, worked toy example, or forecast/"what would happen if" prompt that targets the concept itself. **Partial** = a formula plus a verbal rationale, a control, or an invariance statement, but no picture/toy for the concept. **Formula/code** = definition or a function to implement/call, no development. **Assumed** = named as known prerequisite. **Not taught** = used without definition or source in the page.
- **Coverage** is checked against: original ARENA pages (0.0-0.5, 1.x, 2.x; I also looked at 3.x/4.x grep counts), the FND01-FND06 rows in Spec #39 plus the longer research note `docs/mastery/research/inbox_20261103/foundations.md` (F0-F5), and, for completeness, the PRH01-PRH06 rows in #39 (a separate planned chapter, not FND).
- FND shorthand: FND01 quantities (shapes, units, distances, moments, conditional probability); FND02 objectives (stable log-softmax, CE/KL, declared MSE/cosine, gradients; paired InfoNCE is a "just-in-time" paragraph in #39 and a loss-map row in foundations.md, not in the FND02 row); FND03 PCA (center/SVD/project/reconstruct, projector identities); FND04 t-SNE and UMAP; FND05 metrics (neighbor overlap, trustworthiness/continuity, confusion, probability scores, bins/reliability; ROC AUC and Spearman only as a "choose when needed" menu); FND06 inference (Bonferroni, family manifest, exact nulls; FWER vs FDR; intervals, effect sizes and permutation exchangeability appear in the prose paragraph, not in the row).
- Original 0.0 Prerequisites is a **reading list with five short Q&As** (linear transformations and rank(AB); sum of two normals; derivative of MSE and BCE; cross-entropy under uniform vs exact Q). Entropy, KL, SVD and eigenvalues are only linked readings ("weakly recommended" for SVD). I did not open the 0.0 notebook/solutions, which foundations.md says contain `normalize_rows`, `cos_sim_matrix`, `batched_cross_entropy_loss`.

## 3. Method and limits

Method. (1) Parsed the syllabus and matched 86 pages by title. (2) Searched each tool with hand-tuned regexes over those pages (word-bounded; first corrected after finding that `ROC` and `ridge` matched "process" and "bridge"), recording lessons, first lesson in syllabus order, and hit counts. (3) Read the surrounding passage for the tools marked **R**: KL (7.1, 8.4, 9.1, 11.2, 14.7), entropy (5.2, 5.8, 17.4), cross-entropy (12.1, 11.4, 11.5), temperature (5.6, 5.8, 12.1), logit difference (8.1, 10.1, 16.6), PCA (11.1, 7.4), t-SNE/UMAP/trustworthiness (11.1, 0.6), CCA (11.2), CKA/effective rank (17.4), AUROC definitions (6.2, 7.2, 7.5, 9.1), bootstrap/CI/forking paths (8.1, 10.1), permutation nulls (7.2, 11.1, 6.2), shuffled-label controls (7.3, 10.1, 0.6), Shapley (16.1), influence (16.9, 16.10), LoRA (15.1), InfoNCE (12.1), EMA (14.1), diffusion (13.1-13.3), ELBO (5.8), VQ (13.6), Fourier (5.7), IG (8.2), Pearson/Spearman (7.2, 16.9). (4) Compared against original pages (0.0 Maths section read; 1.3.1 and 1.2 read at a few lines; the rest by headings and counts) and against #39 and foundations.md.

Limits.
- Judgments marked **G** (grep or headings only) are inferences: Mamba/SSM discretization, RoPE, sparse coding, Bayes/posterior, loss landscape, covariance in 9.2/9.6, orthogonal projection outside 11.4, Monte Carlo outside the quoted lines, and least-squares usage.
- "Intuition built" is judged from the page text only. I did not run notebooks, view the rendered figures, or check that saved figures exist; many pages are migrations whose figures are historical. A "Built" verdict means the page contains the pedagogical device, not that a learner succeeds with it.
- Counts are regex lesson counts, not dependency edges; they include references and passing mentions. "Dependent lessons" is a proxy. Section order assumes the syllabus order is the intended learning order.
- Within-lesson forecast/reflect scaffolding is heavy in chapters 5, 11, 14-16 and light in 7, 8, 9.1, 12.x and 17-18 (crude cue count; not a quality measure). That is a pattern, not a per-tool finding.
- Original ARENA coverage for chapters 3-4 was checked by counts only; 0.0's notebook and `solutions.py` were not opened.
- Some syllabus sections point at tools that are not implemented by the page (0.6 L934 states this for selectivity, permutation and multiple-comparison nulls); I report the page, not the syllabus brief.
- No source pages, other issues, or other branches were modified.
